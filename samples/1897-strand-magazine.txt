The Strand Magazine, London, 1897.

A REMARKABLE WHISTLER.

Last evening at St. James's Hall a large audience assembled to hear Mrs. Alice
Shaw, the celebrated American siffleuse, whose whistling has been the talk of two
continents. She whistled selections from the opera with a purity of tone that
many a flautist might envy, and the hall rang with applause. Critics who came to
scoff at the notion of a lady whistler departed warm admirers of her art.
